Meshy AI

URLhttps://www.meshy.ai/ (API docs: docs.meshy.ai)
LicensePaid plans: you own all generated assets, full commercial rights to distribute/sell, no attribution. Free plan: assets are CC BY 4.0 (commercial use requires crediting Meshy). This matters for an app that ships generated assets to end users.
Asset types & formatsText-to-3D + Image-to-3D furniture/props with PBR textures (auto PBR maps), optional auto-rigging. Export FBX, OBJ, GLB, USDZ, STL, BLEND, 3MF — GLB direct-usable in three.js.
Programmatic accessFull REST API (async task create + poll). Credit-based: ~20 credits per generation; free tier ~100 credits/month; paid Pro/Studio tiers. Also exposed via third-party gateways (fal.ai). Cloud-only, not self-hostable.
Render qualityhigh — among the best commercial text/image-to-3D for clean topology + 4K PBR; furniture-quality meshes suitable for real-time scenes, though not photoreal-CAD ultra.
NotesMature market leader, strong furniture/prop suitability. Closed-source, paid for clean commercial rights. Best turnkey on-demand generator for this app.

URLhttps://www.tripo3d.ai/api (devs: developers.tripo3d.ai)

Render qualityhigh — hosted Tripo produces detailed textured meshes competitive with Meshy; self-hosted TripoSR is medium (fast but lower fidelity than the paid pipeline).
NotesStrong all-rounder; the self-hostable TripoSR path is attractive for dev-only/no-cost generation. Furniture suitable.
