3D Asset Sources for Furniture, Fixtures, Materials, Textures & Backgrounds

Exhaustive catalogue of sources for 3D models, PBR materials/textures, HDRI/environment backdrops, scrapable retailer AR models, and academic/industrial datasets — categorised by render quality. Each entry lists name · URL · license · asset types/formats · programmatic-access/scrapability · render quality. IKEA is intentionally excluded (its per-product GLB sitemap-crawl pipeline is already implemented).

111 sources. Quality tiers: Ultra = photoreal/scanned/film-grade; High = production PBR / real product CAD; Medium = decent game-ready / web-AR; Low = flat/low-poly/legacy/CAD-untextured. Segments: free/CC0 models, AI generation + aggregators, scrapable retailers (IKEA-style), premium marketplaces, PBR materials, HDRI/backdrops, academic & furniture datasets.

URLhttps://sketchfab.com/3d-models?features=downloadable (CC0 filter)
LicenseMixed per-asset: CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, CC-BY-NC, etc. Filterable by license. 800k+ free downloadable models; over 1M total.
