# Pet fittings & furniture

> Pet fittings live in the catalog's **Pets** tab, inside the
> [per‑room editor](/room-editor). Open a room to edit it, press <kbd>C</kbd> to
> open the catalog, and pick the **Pets** category tab (or type a pet name — "cat",
> "dog", "litter", "gate" — into the **search box**).

Sofa So Good ships a full set of procedural, to‑scale pet fittings for HDB flats
and condos: the Singapore **compliance** items first, then furniture for cats,
dogs, birds, small pets and fish. Every piece is fully customizable from the
inspector — sizes, styles and colours — like any other parametric item.

## Singapore compliance context

Before the comfort pieces, the Pets tab leads with the fittings that keep a
pet‑friendly home **compliant and safe** in Singapore:

- **Cat Management Framework (CMF).** From 1 September 2024 (with a transition
  period ending **31 August 2026**), HDB households may keep up to **two licensed
  cats**, and every home with a cat must **mesh or screen all windows, grilles,
  balconies and service yards**. The mesh aperture must be small enough to stop a
  cat's head passing through (**≤ 5 cm**) and mounted on the **inside**. The same
  screening also prevents high‑rise falls, which the SPCA links to several cases a
  week.
- **HDB dogs.** An HDB flat may keep **one dog** from the list of approved small
  breeds, so the catalog's crates and beds are sized for small‑to‑medium dogs.

These are design aids, not legal advice — always check the current HDB / AVS
rules before you commit.

## Placing window mesh (snaps to windows)

The **Window mesh screen** is a *window‑bound* fixture: it can only be placed on
a window, and once placed it stays put (you can't drag or rotate it off the
glass). To add it:

1. Open the room and the catalog, then pick **Window mesh screen** from the
   **Pets** tab.
2. Drop it near a window. It **snaps to the nearest window opening** and sizes
   itself to fill the frame — sill to head, the full width.
3. Adjust the **frame colour/finish**, **mesh density** (leave it on **Fine
   (cat‑safe)** for CMF) and **mesh colour** in the inspector.

Repeat for every window, grille bay, balcony and service‑yard opening — that's
the CMF containment requirement. The **Cat window perch** places the same way
(it snaps to a window and rests at sill height) if you want a lookout spot.

## Doorway gates & inserts (snap to doors)

Two *door‑bound* fittings span a doorway and snap to the **nearest door** the
same way the mesh snaps to windows:

- **Pet gate** — spans the opening to keep a pet out of a room (the kitchen, for
  example). Choose the **height**, a **bars** or **mesh** style, a finish, and an
  optional **walk‑through flap** section.
- **Pet door insert** — a flap panel that sits low in the doorway so a cat or dog
  can pass through on its own.

Drop either near a doorway and it snaps across the opening. Like the mesh, a
door‑bound fitting is fixed once placed. The freestanding **Pet playpen**
(4–8 panels) is the non‑doorway alternative for penning a puppy, kitten, rabbit
or small pet in an open area.

## The per‑pet‑type items

After the compliance fittings, the Pets tab is grouped by animal:

- **Dogs (and shared dog/cat pieces).** **Pet bed**, **Orthopedic dog bed**,
  **Dog crate** (XXS–M, wire or wood‑top side‑table styles), **Feeding station**
  (single/double raised bowls), **Dog ramp** (ramp or steps for sofa/bed access),
  **Cooling mat**, and a woven **Pet toy bin**.
- **Cats.** **Cat tree** (2–5 tiers, sisal posts, optional house cube + top
  perch), wall‑mounted **Cat wall shelf / steps / bridge** for a vertical wall
  run, **Scratching post** (vertical / angled / pad), **Litter box** (open,
  covered or top‑entry), a concealing **Litter cabinet**, the **Cat window
  perch**, and a fabric **Cat tunnel**.
- **Birds.** **Bird cage** (dome or rectangular, on a stand or tabletop) and a
  tabletop **Bird play gym**.
- **Small pets.** **Rabbit hutch** (a two‑zone sleeping box + wire run),
  **Small‑pet pen** (a C&C grid pen for guinea pigs), and a glass **Hamster
  enclosure** (≥ 100 × 50 cm at Medium, on the floor or a stand).
- **Fish.** **Aquarium stand + tank** — a load‑rated steel + cabinet stand
  carrying a planted, water‑filled tank (0.6 / 0.9 / 1.2 m). Its inspector note
  reminds you a filled tank is heavy; place it against a wall on solid flooring.

Every item builds in real metres and stays structurally sound, so it reads at
true scale in the room and in walkthrough.

## Tip: furnish the service yard

When you open a **balcony** or **service yard** to edit it, the catalog surfaces
the **Pets** tab near the front — that's the natural home for the litter cabinet,
window mesh, and feeding/toy storage.
