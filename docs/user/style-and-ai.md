# Style helpers & AI

A set of one‑tap helpers restyle your whole home or furnish it for you. Most are
**Pro** tools — switch to Pro from the mode toggle in the **Appearance** popover;
they're hidden in Simple. The AI helpers are **optional and experimental** and use
**your own** model API key, kept in this browser (see [Tips & FAQ](/tips-and-faq)).

## Style quiz

**Tools → Style quiz** (or the command palette, *Style quiz — find your interior
style*) asks a few quick questions and then **applies the matching interior style**
to your home. A fast way to land a coordinated look without picking finishes by
hand. *(Pro mode.)*

## Style transfer

**Tools → Style transfer** (command palette: *Style transfer — restyle every room*)
restyles **every room's floors, walls and palette** to a curated look — Scandi,
Japandi, Industrial and more — in one tap. *(Pro mode.)*

## AI auto-furnish

From the command palette (<kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>), **AI
auto‑furnish (BYO key)** furnishes your rooms from a short **text brief** — describe
the style and who lives there, add your **vision‑model API key** (OpenAI‑compatible,
kept in this browser), and it proposes a layout. It's experimental, so review and
tweak the result. *(Pro mode.)*

## Palette from photo

Also from the command palette, **Palette from photo** extracts a **colour palette
from an image** you choose and maps it onto your room finishes — a quick way to
match a design to a photo you love. *(Pro mode.)*

## More AI tools

Two other AI helpers live with the features they extend:

- **Make photoreal** — restyle a snapshot of your design through a vision model
  (Scandinavian, Japandi, Industrial, and more). See [Budget, checks &
  report](/design-tools).
- **AI wall recognition** — trace walls from a floor‑plan photo. See the [Floor‑plan
  editor](/floor-plan-editor).

Both are experimental and use your own API key.
