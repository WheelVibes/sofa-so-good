# Accounts & cloud sync

By default, Sofa So Good runs entirely on your device and saves your work in the
browser — no account is needed (see [Tips & FAQ](/tips-and-faq)). When the app is
running against its cloud backend, you can also **sign in to sync your designs and
favourites across devices**.

## Signing in

On desktop, open the **Appearance** popover (top‑right of the toolbar) and choose
**Sign in**. On a phone or tablet, the same **Sign in** row is in the main menu
sheet. Once you're signed in, that row reads **Account · ‹your name›**.

- Sign in with your **email and password**. **Accounts are created by an
  administrator** — there is no public sign‑up.
- The sign‑in dialog shows **Signed in as ‹name› · ‹role›** once you're in, with a
  **Sign out** button.

## What syncs

While you're signed in, **your designs and favourites sync to the cloud on this
account**, so you can pick up the same work on another device. Everything still
saves locally as well, so nothing is lost between sessions.

## Admin accounts

Administrator accounts unlock a few extra tools from the same sign‑in dialog:

- **Manage accounts** — add people, and reset a member's password or role
  (there is no public sign‑up, so an admin creates every account).
- **Feature flags** — the developer feature‑flags panel.
- **Shared asset library** — admins browse the curated cloud furniture library
  (served from cloud storage) directly in the catalog; imported items appear in
  the grid alongside the built‑ins. (See [Placing & arranging
  furniture](/placing-furniture) for how catalog items work.)

## Offline & privacy

Some builds of Sofa So Good ship **without** a backend (for example the offline /
GitHub Pages demo). In those builds there is **no sign‑in** — the app runs fully on
your device with no account needed, and the sign‑in entry points are hidden. Your
designs are still saved locally in the browser either way.
